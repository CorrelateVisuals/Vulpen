**The choice**, put to the lead as three options:
- **A, parked:** the engine skips a draw with nothing to draw, and parts given no room write nothing. Everything stays loaded, so it shows again the next frame.
- **B, gone by a param:** a part may say at bind that an output carries nothing, and every node reading it, down the chain, gets no buffers, pipeline, draw, cook or commands. Zero cost, but coming back is a rebuild that makes pipelines again.
- **C:** `size` at least 1; no tabs only by composing the content alone.

The lead leaned A "as it costs so little", asking what carries over to switches between contents ("the one that is off costing near nothing but switching on fast without delay") and to scenes dimmed to 100% black. Those want parked, not gone, so A carries over and B does not. The lead answered **A**.
